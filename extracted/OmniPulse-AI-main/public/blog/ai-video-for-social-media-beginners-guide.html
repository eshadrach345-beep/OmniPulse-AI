AI Video for Social Media: A Beginner’s Guide to Making Short Videos

October 23, 2026 · 6 min read

AI video tools can turn a written idea into a short clip with narration in minutes. They are useful, but they work best when you give them a clear plan. This guide covers a simple workflow and the limits you should know about.

Step 1: Start with one idea and one audience

Write one sentence: “This video helps [who] to [do what].” If you cannot finish that sentence, the video is not ready. A vague idea gives a vague video, whether a human or an AI makes it.

Step 2: Write the hook first

The first two seconds decide whether people keep watching. Good hooks make a promise (“Three mistakes that ruin your photos”), ask a question, or challenge a common belief. Write three versions and pick the strongest.

Step 3: Keep the script short

People speak at roughly 150 words per minute, so a 30-second video is about 75 words. Write a hook, two or three points and one call to action. Read it aloud. If you run out of breath, cut words.

Step 4: Describe visuals in simple terms

AI video models generate short clips, often only a few seconds each, so plan scenes of a few seconds rather than one long take. Describe the subject, the setting, the lighting and the camera movement in plain language. Avoid asking for readable text inside the clip, since generated text is often unreliable. Add captions afterwards instead.

Step 5: Add a natural voice-over

A clear narration carries most of the message. Pick a voice that matches your brand and keep the pace steady. Listen to the whole thing once before you publish and fix any word that sounds wrong.

Step 6: Be open about AI content

Major platforms have rules about labelling AI-generated or altered content, especially when it looks realistic. Read the current policy for each platform you post to and use the labelling option when it applies. Do not depict real people saying things they did not say.

Common mistakes

  • Trying to say too much. One video, one idea.
  • Skipping the review. Always watch the final video before posting.
  • Posting once and giving up. Your tenth video will be much better than your first.
