How to Find Trending Topics in Your Niche Before Everyone Else

October 23, 2026 · 5 min read

Most creators find out about a trend when it is already everywhere. By then, the people who spotted it early have collected the views, and you are one of a thousand accounts making the same video. The fix is not luck. It is a short routine you can run every week.

Start with where trends show up first

Trends rarely begin on the biggest accounts. They show up in smaller places first, so check these:

  • Search suggestions. Type the start of a phrase in TikTok, YouTube and Instagram search. What the platform suggests is what people are looking for right now.
  • Google Trends. Search your niche keyword and look at the “rising” related queries. A query that is climbing fast is a better target than one that is already huge.
  • Comment sections. Open the three most recent posts from the biggest creators in your niche. Repeated questions in the comments are topics nobody has answered well yet.
  • Newsletters and news in your field. A new product, rule change or event is a reason for people to search and watch this week.

Check that the trend is real and recent

Before you build a video around something, run three quick checks:

  1. Recency. Sort results by newest. If the top posts are from months ago, the moment has passed.
  2. Spread. Is it showing up from several unrelated accounts, or only one? One account is a post, not a trend.
  3. Room for a new angle. If fifty people have made the same video, you need a different take: a counter-opinion, a beginner version, a mistake to avoid, or a result from your own experience.

Turn it into a video quickly

Speed matters more than polish. Write one clear hook, three to five short points, and one call to action. If you can publish within a day or two of spotting the topic, you are early. If it takes two weeks, you are probably late.

Keep a simple topic log

Write down every topic you spot, the date, and where you saw it. After a month you will notice which sources give you the earliest signals for your niche. Drop the ones that are always late.
